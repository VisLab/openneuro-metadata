This is the entire PET dataset for the [18F]CHDI-385 study in zQ175DN mice, including Baseline, Mass effect, Test-Retest variability and 3 months evaluation. Session 1 - Baseline at 9 months; Session 2 - Mass effect at 9 months; Session 3 - Mass effect at 9 months (Second scan); Session 4 - Retest at 9 months; Session 5 - Baseline at 3 months. Connection loss during a few scans in Session 5 led to reduced counts in one or two frames, which were subsequently excluded during data processing to mitigate the impact. All affected scan files and corresponding IDIF files made available are not corrected for this connection loss and contain all frames.
