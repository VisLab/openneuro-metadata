This dataset consists of the fMRI data from a total of 68 participants when they listening a 10 mins story in Chinese, a 10 mins story in Mongolian (MG) and in resting state. Due to the excessive head motion 4 participants were exclueded for each of the three conditions from the further analysis. As reported in Liu, Lanfang, Jiang Jiahao, Hehui Li and Guosheng Ding (2024). Tripartite organization of brain state dynamics underlying spoken narrative comprehension. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.99997.1
